Flip to Shhh is an open-source, privacy-first, ultra-lightweight background utility designed to bring Google Pixel's signature Flip to Shhh (Flip to Do Not Disturb) gesture to all non-Pixel Android 13+ devices (Samsung One UI, Xiaomi HyperOS, OPPO ColorOS, vivo OriginOS, OnePlus OxygenOS, Motorola, etc.).

Simply place your phone face down flat on a desk or table for 2 seconds. The app instantly activates Do Not Disturb (DND) mode with crisp dual-pulse haptic feedback and optional screen locking. Picking up or flipping the phone face-up automatically restores normal ringer settings within 300ms.

Features:
• Multi-sensor spatial gravity vector & tilt angle analysis
• Optical proximity detection with virtual sensor fallback
• 2.0s continuous table stillness window with physiological hand tremor filtering
• Biometric-friendly screen lock via native Accessibility Service
• Smart DND ownership tracking (never overrides existing scheduled quiet hours)
• 100% offline with ZERO network permissions (INTERNET permission omitted)
• Samsung One UI / Material You inspired dynamic palette adaptation
• Fully localized in English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese
