# Flip to Shhh — App Icon Design Brief

## App Concept
A Samsung Galaxy flagship utility that auto-enables Do Not Disturb + silent mode when the phone is placed face-down. The name "Flip to Shhh" combines the physical action (flip) with the onomatopoeia of silence (shhh).

## Icon Requirements
- **Format**: 1024×1024 PNG (will be converted to Adaptive Icon)
- **Style**: Flat or subtle gradient, Material You / One UI design language
- **Background**: Dark (#0F0F10 or very dark grey), NOT white/light
- **Accent**: Cyan-blue (#80D8FF) as primary accent, matching app UI
- **Safe zone**: Keep all important elements within center 70% (Adaptive Icon mask will crop edges on different launchers)
- **No text**: The icon should communicate without letters
- **Scalable**: Must remain recognizable at 48×48px (notification bar size)

## Design Direction
The icon should visually communicate: **phone + flip + silence**

Preferred concepts (in priority order):
1. A phone shown from the back (camera module visible) at a slight tilt, with a soft glowing ring suggesting DND/silence
2. Sound waves hitting a phone barrier and being cancelled (× or fading)
3. A minimalist phone silhouette mid-flip (rotated 90°), with a small silence indicator

## Tone
- Premium, minimal, confident
- NOT cute, NOT cartoonish
- Should look at home next to Samsung's native One UI app icons
- Subtle depth is OK (soft shadows, inner glow) but avoid skeuomorphism

## Color Palette
- Background: #0F0F10 (near-black)
- Primary accent: #80D8FF (cyan-blue)
- Secondary: #E2E2E6 (off-white for phone body)
- Glow/highlight: #80D8FF at 30-50% opacity

## What to Avoid
- No stock notification bell icons
- No literal "shhh" lips/finger gesture (too cartoonish at small sizes)
- No complex scenes (won't scale to 48px)
- No text/letters
- No gradients that clash with Dynamic Color theming
